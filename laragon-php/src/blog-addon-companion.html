Scan with us

Two downloads, no account

Since day one, every number on this site has come from scans: a small add-on running /who in the background while someone plays. For most of that time, that someone was me, along with a few friends willing to run a test build. That's why some realms have weeks of history and others barely show up.

Starting today, anyone can help. The WoWCensus add-on and the WoWCensus Companion app are both free to download. Together they let you add what you see in game to the census on wowcensus.io. You don't have to change how you play.

  • Download the WoWCensus add-on
  • Download the WoWCensus Companion (Windows, macOS and Linux)

The add-on

Runs while you play

The add-on works on Retail, Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, MoP Classic and WoW: Forever. It starts when you log in and takes one step through its /who list each time you move or click in the world. WoW only allows /who after real player input, so it never runs while you're idle, and you'll hardly notice it.

  • /wc or the minimap button opens the window and shows how far the scan has got.
  • If it asks for your region, type /wc region us (or eu, kr, tw, cn).
  • The Auto: On / Off button pauses it. Right-clicking the minimap button does the same.

The add-on only records what /who already shows anyone: names, realms, races, classes, levels and guilds. On its own, that data stays in your SavedVariables on your computer.

The Companion

Sends it to the site

The Companion is the piece that gets your scans to wowcensus.io. Run it once and it finds your World of Warcraft folders by itself. If the add-on isn't installed yet, it offers to install it for every game version you have. Click Turn on uploads, and from then on it sits in the system tray and sends new data each time you log out or /reload.

  • No account, password or token. Just turn uploads on.
  • Only what's new. The first upload is your whole save. After that it only sends what changed.
  • You can see what happened. The Activity tab shows each upload, and receipts change to "On wowcensus.io" once your data is live on the site.
  • Your language. The app is available in English, Korean, Spanish, German, French and Italian.

It never sends your Battle.net login, your WoW account folder name or file paths from your computer. Uploads stay off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off again in Settings at any time.

Your other add-ons

Off unless you opt in

Some questions can't be answered from /who. How long does an item actually take to sell? Is it cheaper to buy it from the Auction House or from a vendor? What drops where? Add-ons like Journalator, TradeSkillMaster, Auctionator and Dungeon Journal already log this about your own play.

If you want, you can share that too. Tick Collect other addon data in the add-on (or type /wc market on), then tick the add-ons you want under Other add-ons in the Companion's Settings. Other players' names, mail and chat text are removed on your computer before anything is sent, and only the last 90 days are kept. This is what powers the drop rates and sell times on item pages, so every log helps.

Why it matters

More eyes, better stories

One player scanning one realm at one time of day only sees part of the picture. The census gets better with every person who runs it: more realms, more hours of the day, both factions, every region. When your realm looks empty on the site, it usually just means nobody there has scanned yet. You can change that.

If something doesn't work, leave a comment below or find us on Discord. Thanks for helping. I can't wait to see what your realms look like.
