A page for every character

Rolling out across every edition

Last week WoW: Forever characters got their own pages on WoWCensus. Now the rest of the game is getting them too. Character pages are rolling out across every edition we track, from Classic Era and TBC Anniversary to Retail.

Each page pulls from Blizzard's character profiles. You'll see what someone has equipped, with item levels and enchants, plus their talents, professions, stats, reputations, PvP record, achievements and when they last logged in. Click any piece of gear to see its item page and who else is wearing it. Guild rosters link to members who have a page, so you can see who you're up against in a guild, or who you play alongside.

This is happening gradually. Pages show up as we fetch each character's profile, a realm at a time, so if you can't find yours yet, it's on the way. Some characters won't get a page, because Blizzard doesn't publish every profile.

As always, this is about the bigger picture. A single character page is nice to have, but thousands of them show what a realm is actually wearing, what it's specced into and what it's crafting. That's the part I'm most excited about.
