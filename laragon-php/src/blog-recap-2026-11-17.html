A streamer-fueled gold farm in Blackmaw Hold is the talk of WoW: Forever this morning. Blizzard quietly hotfixed it, but players want to know whether the gold will be removed. Elsewhere, Sunday's hotfixes reworked Windfury Totem, and today's maintenance brings the long-awaited Auction House and mail changes.

Blackmaw Hold Gold Exploit

Groups found that a room of non-elite mobs in Blackmaw Hold could be farmed for gold quickly, with Output Lag estimating up to about 160 gold an hour. Blizzard has since hotfixed the farm but hasn't said whether gold earned from it will be rolled back or whether anyone will be banned. That has players worried about early inflation, as PC Gamer reports. The official forums now have a mega thread on the exploit, along with calls for the exploit to have real consequences.

Hotfixes & Patch Notes

November 16

Blizzard's November 16 hotfixes touch several classes and some content (Wowhead summary):

  • Windfury Totem attacks now respect armor, have an internal cooldown, and generate Rage.
  • Cleave and Heroic Strike now proc correctly for Fury Warriors.
  • Fixes went in for Rogue poisons, Druid Wild Growth, and Pounce bleeds.
  • The Drowned City boss is no longer immune to stuns.
  • The repeatable Water Pouch Bounty quest in Tanaris no longer awards experience.

Also arriving with today's maintenance, Vampiric Embrace's party healing drops from 20% to 10% of Shadow damage dealt. The caster's own healing is doubled, so Blizzard says solo play is unchanged (Icy Veins).

Auction House & Mail Changes Today

With today's maintenance

After being delayed last week, the Auction House minimum deposit drops from 1 silver to 1 copper with today's maintenance. Mail delivery also speeds up from roughly an hour to roughly two minutes, and the mislabeled "Stormwind Auction House" mail is fixed (Bind on Forever).

Population & Faction Balance

NA PvP realms

Horde character creation remains locked on North American PvP realms, and Blizzard hasn't given a date for reopening it (Wowhead). On X, some players are asking Blizzard to rethink layering, saying it is killing world PvP.
