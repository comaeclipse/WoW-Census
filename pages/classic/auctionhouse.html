No auction scan uploaded for this faction yet. In game, run /ml scan at the auction house, /reload, then upload with upload-realm.ps1 -Flavor classic-era.

An auction house scan observes listings, not sales. Unit price is the weighted median buyout asked for an item, and listed value is quantity × that price — what sellers want, not what anyone paid.
Listings priced absurdly above the rest of the market are excluded from the totals and top lists, and named in their own panel rather than dropped quietly.
Change compares listed quantity with the preceding scan; it is net supply movement, not sales.
Items a scan could not name appear by id. Companion to the MarketLens addon.
