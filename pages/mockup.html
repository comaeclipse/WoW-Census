Font mockup

The site keeps its identity: Press Start 2P for the logo and VT323 for body text. The proposal adds Warcraft-style headings and swaps the 7–9px Press Start labels, which are hard to read at that size, for a pixel face that holds up small. Flip the switch to compare the same page section both ways.
